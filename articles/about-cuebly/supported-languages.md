# Supported languages

The Cuebly platform and the Cuebly app are available in 10 languages:

| Language | Shown as |
|---|---|
| Czech | Čeština |
| Danish | Dansk |
| Dutch | Nederlands |
| English | English |
| French | Français |
| German | Deutsch |
| Italian | Italiano |
| Spanish | Español |
| Swedish | Svenska |
| Turkish | Türkçe |

## Change the language

- **Platform:** choose a language in the language list at the bottom of the menu. The language of a customer environment is set per account by the account manager; each user can change it for themselves.
- **App:** open **Settings** in the app and choose a language. The app language does not depend on the language of the phone.

Texts in the app come from the platform. A corrected text reaches every app without an app update; restart the app to see it.

## Missing your language?

New languages are added on request. Contact your Cuebly account manager.
