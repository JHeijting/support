# Emerit

Emerit devices are made by Magneta. They are fully integrated with Cuebly: you configure them from the Cuebly platform.

## Models

| Model | Type |
|---|---|
| [e-WG100](/i/16) | Personal alarm |
| [e-WG200](/i/111) | Personal alarm |
| [Emerit Connect App](/i/89) | App for Android and iOS that connects a small Bluetooth button (SOS, no-motion and tilt alarm) |

## Connect to the platform

For the best result, add your Emerit device to Cuebly and configure it there, not in the Emerit Console. Every configuration option of the device is available in the Cuebly platform.

The e-WG100 and e-WG200 connect through the Emerit Console:

1. Connect the device to the Emerit Console with the Emerit quick start guide.
2. In the Emerit Console, enable **Alarm monitoring server**.
3. Set the alarm monitoring IP server to **app.cuebly.com**, port **8486**.

The model pages list the events to send and the tracking settings. The Emerit Connect App has its own steps on its page.
