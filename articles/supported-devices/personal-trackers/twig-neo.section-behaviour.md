## How this device behaves

The Twig Neo sends an alarm in two steps: the indoor location (beacons) follows after the first alarm message. How recent that indoor location is depends on the setting **show beacons in display**. See [How Twig devices behave](/en/supported-devices/personal-trackers/twig#how-twig-devices-behave) and [How location reaches an alarm](/en/supported-devices/location-in-alarms).
