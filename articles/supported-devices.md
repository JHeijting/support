# Supported devices

Cuebly works with personal alarms, gateways and the Cuebly app. Each brand has its own page with what all its models share, and a page per model.

## By type

- [Personal alarms](/i/4): small wearable devices that raise alarms, share their location and often support two-way voice.
- [Gateways](/i/84): devices that pass on alarms from other systems, such as I/O gateways for machinery or ESPA 4.4.4 gateways for a fire control panel.
- The [Cuebly app](/i/29) turns a phone into an alarm device.
